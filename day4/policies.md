# CityCare Clinic Policies

## Cancellation Policy

Appointments should be cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment time.

If an appointment is cancelled less than 24 hours before the scheduled appointment, the clinic may apply its late cancellation policy.

## Late Arrival Policy

Patients should arrive on time for their appointments.

A patient arriving more than 15 minutes late may need to reschedule the appointment depending on doctor availability.
